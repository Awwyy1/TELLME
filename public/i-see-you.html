I see you

Being seen means someone notices the real you — not just your grades, your mistakes or your usefulness, but your inner life.

Children who weren't seen often become adults who are very good at taking care of everyone else and very bad at asking for anything. These letters notice what usually goes unnoticed: the effort, the depth, the quiet weight you carry.
