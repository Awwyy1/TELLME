I'm proud of you

Some people grow up hearing “I'm proud of you” so often it becomes background noise. Others wait their whole lives for it — through graduations, new jobs, hard-won recoveries — and it never comes.

If you're in the second group, you probably learned to measure your worth in achievements, hoping the next one would finally earn those words. These letters say them without conditions: not for what you've done, but for who you've been all along.
