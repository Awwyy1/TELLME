Terms of Use

tellme.mom is a free emotional wellness tool designed to offer words of comfort, love, and affirmation. By using this site, you agree to the following.

Not a substitute for therapy

This site is not therapy. The messages generated here are created by an AI and are not a substitute for professional mental health support. If you are experiencing a crisis, depression, grief, or any form of emotional distress that feels overwhelming, please reach out to a qualified mental health professional or a crisis helpline in your country. You can find free, confidential helplines worldwide at findahelpline.com.

No claims

The words on this site are meant to offer comfort — not diagnosis, not advice, not treatment. We make no claims about therapeutic outcomes. You use this site at your own discretion. We are not responsible for how the content affects you emotionally. If at any point the experience feels difficult, please step away and take care of yourself first.

For younger visitors

If you are under 18, we gently recommend using this site together with a trusted adult — a relative, a teacher, a counselor, or anyone you feel safe with. This site generates emotionally sensitive content, and having someone beside you can make the experience feel safer.

Privacy

We do not collect personal data. We do not require registration. We do not store your name, email, location, or any identifying information.

When you request a message, your selected theme is sent to Anthropic's API — the AI that generates the response. This transmission is subject to Anthropic's own privacy policy at anthropic.com. We do not log, save, or have access to what was generated for you.

To keep the service free and protect it from abuse, we count how many letters are requested from each connection. We never store your IP address itself — only a one-way, irreversible hash of it, and these counters delete themselves automatically within about a day. A Cloudflare Turnstile check may run in the background to confirm requests come from a person rather than a bot; it is subject to Cloudflare's privacy policy. Like any website, our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps short-lived technical server logs, which can include IP addresses, for security and reliability.

No cookies are used for tracking. We use Vercel Web Analytics to collect anonymous, aggregated page view data (no personal information, no behavior tracking, no user identification). This site exists to be a safe place — and that includes how we handle your data.
