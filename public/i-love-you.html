I love you unconditionally

Unconditional love is supposed to be the starting point — the floor beneath everything else. But for many of us, love came with conditions: be good, be quiet, be successful, don't need too much.

Growing up that way teaches you that love has to be earned and can be taken away. These letters offer the other kind, the kind that doesn't depend on your behaviour, your mood, or your achievements.
