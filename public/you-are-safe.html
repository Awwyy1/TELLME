You are safe with me

Feeling safe isn't only about locked doors. It's about having somewhere you can be upset, wrong, scared or messy without paying for it later.

If home didn't feel like that, your body may still be on alert years later — bracing, anticipating, keeping the peace. These letters describe a place where you can finally put that guard down, even if only for a moment.
