You can do hard things

Sometimes what we need most isn't advice but someone who believes we can make it. A mother saying “you can do this” before an exam, an interview, a diagnosis or a goodbye is a small thing that carries enormous weight.

If nobody said it to you, you've probably been getting through hard things alone. These letters are a reminder of how much you've already survived — and that you don't have to be fearless to keep going.
